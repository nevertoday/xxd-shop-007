**"Take the product apart" for shoppers in real assembly order, with every part saying what it is and what it solves.**
The image between an engineering drawing and AI fantasy: attractive, and not made up.

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **You give** | A product photo (required); exploded views, drawings, a manual or a parts list (the fuller, the more accurate); specs such as power or battery life |
| **You get** | Teardown posters for the detail-page hero, key-benefit page and tech page; also a three-page set |
| **The look** | Clean light background · a centred exploded view · fine lines, numbers and short labels · a few info cards · benefit icons along the bottom |
| **Best for** | Electronics, appliances, personal-care devices and tools whose pages need to show what is inside |

> **Once installed, just say:**
>
> /xxd-shop-007 Here's the robot vacuum and its official exploded view; parts are top cover, lidar, main board, battery, fan, side brush, main brush, dustbin — make a teardown for the detail page

## What it solves

Pages for electronics and appliances always need a "what's inside" image. It is usually an engineering drawing too stiff to read, or an AI scatter of glowing chips that looks good and is invented.

**Shop 007** makes the image in between: **a premium e-commerce infographic plus industrial-design presentation**. The product explodes along its real assembly order; fine lines, numbers and short labels say what each part is and what it solves; 3–5 benefits close at the bottom.

## Where the parts list comes from

| You provide | How it is taken apart |
|---|---|
| Exploded view, drawings, manual, parts list | Followed exactly — your order and your names |
| Unclear module names or principles | Checked against official and professional sources first |
| Only an exterior photo | Only the shell and visible modules; the inside stays a restrained sketch with no invented part names |

The list and its sources go to `facts.md` in the task folder, and every label matches it. Manuals and PDFs are read and turned into the list; they are not sent to the image model as references.

## Teardown set

Ask for a set (3 by default) and you get the three page types a detail page uses:

- **Hero:** the whole product exploded, showing the overall assembly;
- **Benefit page:** one key module and the problem it solves;
- **Tech page:** a partial cutaway with one principle card.

Each page labels only its share of the parts list; the key benefits appear on the hero only. Without inside information it makes fewer pages rather than inventing modules.

## What to prepare

- **A product photo** (required): it keeps the product recognisable.
- **Structure information** (recommended): the closer to "exploded view + parts list", the more accurate.
- **Specs** (optional): battery life, power, suction, resolution — numbers only come from you.

## How to use it

### Step 1: install (once)

```bash
git clone https://github.com/nevertoday/xxd-shop-007.git
mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills
ln -s "$(pwd)/xxd-shop-007" ~/.claude/skills/xxd-shop-007
```

Codex users link the same folder into `~/.codex/skills/xxd-shop-007`. Restart your AI assistant's session after installing.

### Step 2: say what you want in plain words

```text
/xxd-shop-007 Here's the robot vacuum and its official exploded view; parts are top cover, lidar, main board, battery, fan, side brush, main brush, dustbin — make a teardown for the detail page
/xxd-shop-007 I only have an exterior photo of this electric toothbrush — make a teardown
/xxd-shop-007 Manual PDF attached — make a 3-page teardown set
```

No flags to memorise. Anything you leave open, such as sizes or how many, is asked in one go, and next time you can reuse your previous settings.

### Step 3: what it does next

1. Reads your product photo and description and asks only what is still open; sizes are recommended from the use, with pixel dimensions.
2. It first builds the parts list, with a source on every row.
3. For a set, you first see the storyboard and the hero; the rest follow after you confirm.
4. Every size is composed afresh, never cropped from another image.
5. Checks each image before delivery: is the product unchanged, is the text spelled right, are there numbers you never gave? Anything that fails is reported and redone.
6. Saves PNGs to a new task folder under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-shop-007/` and gives you the path.

## FAQ

**Can I have no text?**

Yes, but it becomes an unlabelled exploded render and loses its infographic role; it tells you first and proceeds once you confirm.

**Can it make English detail pages?**

Yes — ask for English text and the header bar switches to English too.

**What do I need?**

Three things: ① an AI assistant that supports Skills, such as Claude Code or Codex; ② an image-capable model — your assistant's built-in image generation, or a model provider configured in Codex that generates images through the Responses API (GPT Image 2 by default); ③ Python 3.11+ with Pillow (`pip install pillow`).

**Does it cost anything? Can I use it commercially?**

The Skill is free to download; image generation is billed to your own model account. The project uses the PolyForm Noncommercial licence: personal learning, research and testing are fine; running a shop, client work or other commercial use needs the author's written permission first — see "License" at the end.

**How large are the images?**

You can ask for a ratio or exact pixels. The real size is whatever the model returns — never secretly upscaled — and the file name states the true pixels.

**Will the text in the image have typos?**

AI images occasionally misspell words or add small marks such as ® next to a brand name. Every image is zoomed and checked before delivery, and any failure is redone; all samples on this page passed that check.

**Will it invent prices or sales numbers?**

No. Anything that looks like a fact — price, specs, origin, sales, awards, certifications — comes only from what you provide or text clearly readable on the product; otherwise it is left out, with no "¥XX" placeholder.

**Can it handle many products at once?**

Yes. Give it a folder of product photos: each image is one product, sizes and text are asked once, and no product's facts or copy leak into another's.

## Principles

- **The style stays true:** behind this Skill is a mature commercial design prompt ([original brief](references/original-prompt/zh-CN.md) · [English reading translation](references/original-prompt/en.md)). The Skill never rewrites it or adds its own colours or art direction; it only hands the model your product, sizes, text and facts.
- **The product is not redesigned:** with a product photo, shape, colour, packaging text and logo follow the photo.
- **Only your facts,** and each product stands alone — nothing borrowed from other products, earlier results or samples.
- **Real PNG bitmaps,** never SVG or code drawings passed off as images; every run creates a new task folder.
- Label names come only from the parts list, and every row of the list has a source.
